It brings sources, corpus, witness, history, people, events, stories, research, evidence, ontology, creative work, journeys, and accountable action into one coherent experience while keeping their identities and ownership distinct.

## Mission and promise

**Proposed mission:** help people and communities preserve knowledge, inspect its sources, understand context and uncertainty, connect ideas responsibly, and make accountable decisions.

**User promise:** a person can move from a source to a traceable passage or record, examine related evidence and interpretations, contribute or review work under clear rights and responsibility, and understand what is known, uncertain, private, or awaiting approval.

## Product principles

- Preserve first; source every claim; respect context.
- Evidence precedes conclusions. Provenance precedes publication.
- Identity precedes attribution. Context precedes comparison.
- Human responsibility precedes automation.
- Privacy, rights and uncertainty remain visible.
- Reuse knowledge, not unnecessary application boundaries.
- Keep one product experience with explicit internal domain ownership.
- Never present a designed, proposed, implemented, tested, verified, deployed, or production-certified state as another.

## Product journey

```text
PRESERVE → DISCOVER → TRACE → CONNECT → UNDERSTAND → RESEARCH
→ CREATE → REVIEW → PUBLISH → ACT → PRESERVE FOR THE FUTURE
```

## Editorial and spiritual boundaries

The product may express the project's values of honoring ALLAH, respecting the Prophets Muhammad ﷺ and Isa عليه السلام, and upholding knowledge, honesty, trust, justice and responsibility. Product narrative is not revelation. AI has no religious authority or final decision power. Interpretation must be attributed. Historical claims require sources and confidence/uncertainty. A theological concept is not a universal fact merely because it appears in the application.

Fictional characters must be clearly separated from real people, prophets, angels, and executable AI agents. Story is not automatically history. Exact source text, translations, commentary and interpretation are separate objects with attribution and rights.

## Audience and capabilities

Initial audience segments and their priority remain **PENDING owner validation**. Candidate capabilities include a source-aware reader, corpus discovery, entity/event explorer, research workspace, evidence review, creative/story workspace, publishing workflow, personal journey and accountable actions. ## Non-goals for the initial foundation

- Recreate every historic product or repository.
- Operate multiple independent MoonWitness applications, portals, backends or identities.
- Import every dataset before rights and mapping are verified.
- Treat AI output as a canonical write or authoritative verdict.
- Add infrastructure based only on its presence in an old repository.
- Claim production readiness before deployment, recovery, security and acceptance evidence exist.
