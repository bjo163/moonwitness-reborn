## 1. Purpose and authority

This constitution establishes the canonical identity, public narrative, principles and ecosystem relationship for A-L-L-A-H.COM and MoonWitness. It is the single reference for the public-facing identity of EMPIRE OF THE LIGHT — A-L-L-A-H.

It does not silently ratify every proposed feature, technical decision, dataset, migration, integration or production claim. Vision is direction; implementation must be verified. Technical ownership and delivery status remain governed by the canonical architecture documents, ADR registry, TODO.md, tests and release evidence.

### Status vocabulary
- **CANONICAL** — approved identity, wording or boundary defined here.
- **PROPOSED** — candidate capability or design requiring a separate decision.
- **IMPLEMENTED** — present in the repository at a stated commit.
- **TESTED** — relevant automated or manual tests passed.
- **VERIFIED** — evidence inspected and recorded.
- **DEPLOYED** — running in a named environment.
- **PRODUCTION-CERTIFIED** — applicable operational, security, recovery and acceptance gates passed.

No status implies the next.

## 3. Relationship between A-L-L-A-H and MoonWitness

A-L-L-A-H.COM is the ecosystem identity and public gateway. MoonWitness is the primary software platform and implementation foundation in this repository.

MoonWitness is one integrated platform for preserving, discovering, tracing, connecting, understanding, researching, creating, reviewing, publishing and acting on knowledge. It is not defined solely as a chatbot, scripture reader, social network, dashboard, ERP or religious application.

- **A-L-L-A-H.COM / EMPIRE OF THE LIGHT:** ecosystem identity, public vision and gateway.
- **MoonWitness:** integrated Web/API platform and product experience.
- **Domains and addons:** internally owned capabilities with explicit data and service boundaries.
- **External or historical projects:** independent sources or integrations unless an owner-approved decision assigns a specific role and contract.

The ecosystem name does not make every historical repository, product, dataset or experiment a MoonWitness subsystem.

## 5. Core principles

1. **Knowledge before assumption.** Preserve sources and respect context.
2. **Evidence before conclusion.** Distinguish facts, interpretations, hypotheses, opinions and fiction.
3. **Provenance before publication.** Readers should be able to inspect where a claim or record came from.
4. **Human responsibility before automation.** AI assists; people retain accountability and decision authority.
5. **Faith with respect and integrity.** Express the project's values without claiming divine authority for software or AI.
6. **Privacy and rights by design.** Access, attribution, permitted use, retention and deletion must be explicit.
7. **Creativity with purpose.** Stories invite reflection without impersonating evidence or history.
8. **Progress through deeds.** Measure progress through responsible, verifiable actions rather than promises.
9. **One experience, clear ownership.** Reuse capabilities without duplicating canonical data or domain authority.
10. **Honest engineering.** Never describe a proposal as implemented, a test as production proof, or a deployment as certification.

## 6. Knowledge, evidence and editorial integrity

The platform must keep these content types distinct: original source and metadata; edition, version and exact text; translation; commentary and tafsir; scholarly or historical interpretation; research claim and supporting evidence; user annotation and personal reflection; AI-generated suggestion; fictional character, story and creative work.

A translation is not the original text. Commentary is not the source. Personal reflection is not a universal finding. A story is not automatically history. AI output is not automatically verified.

Claims about history, science, people, events or the natural world require traceable sources and honest uncertainty. Rights and attribution must be checked before import, display, indexing, export or publication.

## 7. Spiritual and religious boundary

The project's identity may honor ALLAH and express values such as knowledge, honesty, trust, justice, compassion and responsibility.

- Software, AI, a model, a character or a website has no divine authority.
- AI must not present itself as revelation, a prophet, an angel, a religious authority or a final arbiter of faith.
- Qur'anic text, translations, tafsir and personal reflections must be separately attributed and represented.
- Religious interpretations must be attributed to their sources and not passed off as universally settled when differences exist.
- Fictional characters and narrative devices must remain distinct from real people and religious figures.
- Numerical coincidences, dates, times, astronomical events or personal experiences must not be asserted as divine messages or proof without legitimate evidence.

The public website must communicate this boundary respectfully and plainly.

Initial audience segments, launch audience, success metrics and first dataset remain subject to the open decisions in docs/product/PRODUCT_SPECIFICATION.md and TODO.md.

Candidate capabilities include source-aware reading and corpus discovery; people, event and entity exploration; research questions, claims and evidence review; creative and story workspaces; contribution, review and publishing workflows; personal learning journeys and accountable actions; and controlled, source-aware AI assistance.

A candidate capability is not automatically approved for the first release. Prioritize a small complete user journey over a large collection of incomplete screens or modules.

## 10. Public website narrative and information architecture

The A-L-L-A-H.COM homepage should tell one coherent story:

1. **Opening — Enter the Empire of the Light.** State the identity and promise without exaggerated claims.
2. **The Vision — A connected world of learning and action.** Explain why knowledge, technology and responsibility belong together.
3. **The Ecosystem — One vision, clear roles.** Explain A-L-L-A-H.COM and MoonWitness, and introduce experience names only with accurate status labels.
4. **The Journey — Preserve to act.** Show the source-to-action journey and distinguish present capability from future direction.
5. **MoonWitness — The platform foundation.** Explain what the current software actually does, based on verified implementation.
6. **Knowledge, Research and Stories.** Show the difference between sources, evidence, interpretations and creative work.
7. **Trust and Responsibility.** Explain privacy, rights, provenance, human review and AI boundaries.
8. **The Declaration — Proven through deeds.** Close with the canonical declaration and a concrete, honest call to action.

Suggested primary navigation: Home; Vision; Ecosystem; MoonWitness; Knowledge; Research; Stories; Principles.

Only publish routes and feature descriptions that exist or are clearly labelled as future direction. Do not present mockups, roadmap items or external projects as live services.

## 11. Visual and verbal identity

The public identity should feel distinctive, cinematic and memorable while remaining legible, accessible and trustworthy.

Creative direction may use a cosmic/night-sky atmosphere, moon imagery, deep dark surfaces, restrained antique-gold and crimson accents, futuristic architecture and editorial typography. This is a creative direction, not a frozen token specification. Final tokens, typography, iconography, accessibility, responsive behavior and light/dark themes belong to MASTER_DESIGN.md.

The writing voice should be confident, human, clear and grounded. It may be poetic in the hero and declaration, but explanatory sections must use concrete language. Avoid grandiose claims about power, omniscience, AGI, spiritual status or completed capabilities.
