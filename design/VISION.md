# A-L-L-A-H.COM — Product Vision

**Status:** Foundation direction  
**Scope:** Public front door and connected product ecosystem  
**Audience:** First-time visitors, returning members, contributors, creators, researchers, and authorized operators

## 1. North star

A-L-L-A-H.COM is the public front door to the **Empire of the Light**: a coherent digital world where visitors can understand the purpose of the project, encounter its stories and transmissions, discover the connected spaces, and choose a meaningful next step.

The experience should feel like entering a living world, not opening a conventional company website. Its distinctiveness comes from the project's own narrative, symbols, language, visual grammar, and useful destinations—not from visual effects alone.

The design is intended to honor and direct attention toward ALLAH through humility, gratitude, reflection, truthful storytelling, and beneficial action. The interface must never claim to speak for ALLAH, promise divine approval, or present subjective interpretations as certain divine messages.

## 2. Product promise

**A world to enter. Stories to receive. Knowledge to examine. Good work to contribute.**

The Home page must answer, quickly and honestly:
1. What is A-L-L-A-H.COM?
2. What can I explore here?
3. Where do MoonWitness, Dock, Cockpit, and Headquarter fit?
4. What can I do without an account?
5. What requires sign-in or a specific permission?
6. How do I distinguish a story, reflection, hypothesis, and verified fact?

## 3. Product ecosystem

### A-L-L-A-H.COM Home — The Gate
Public orientation, narrative introduction, discovery, and clear routes into the ecosystem. It is not an admin dashboard and does not absorb the applications below.

### MoonWitness — The Transmission & Story Universe
Narrative chapters, transmissions, creative works, timelines, archives, and contextual research. It is the story-bearing voice of the ecosystem.

### Dock — The Community Harbor
Community participation, shared discussion, contribution discovery, and member-facing social spaces. It is not the operational control center.

### Cockpit — The Personal Workspace
A member's own working environment: personal tools, AI interaction, saved work, preferences, and personal controls. It is not the public landing page.

### Headquarter — The Spatial Command Center
Ecosystem-level coordination, mission control, knowledge operations, governance, and authorized system observability. It is not the community feed or personal workspace.

### Moon UI — The Shared Interface Language
Shared tokens, components, patterns, accessibility conventions, and interaction primitives. It is not a separate user destination.

## 4. Primary audiences and needs

| Audience | Primary need | First useful outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Curious visitor | Understand the project without being overwhelmed | Understand the purpose and find one relevant destination |
| Returning reader | Resume a story or transmission | Continue from a real archive item |
| Community participant | Find where and how to participate | Reach Dock and understand participation rules |
| Creator/contributor | Understand how to contribute work | Find the contribution path and its requirements |
| Research-minded visitor | Inspect sources and distinguish evidence from interpretation | Open a sourced research or knowledge item |
| Member | Reach personal tools and saved work | Enter Cockpit |
| Authorized operator | Reach ecosystem coordination | Enter Headquarter if authorized |

Do not assume every visitor is a member, every member is an operator, or every user has the same permissions.

## 5. Experience principles

1. **Reverence without spectacle:** sacred references are treated with care; avoid turning worship, scripture, or divine names into decorative UI.
2. **Truth before atmosphere:** a cinematic presentation never outranks accurate provenance, honest status, and clear meaning.
3. **Invitation, not pressure:** discovery is open and calm. No manipulative urgency, guilt, forced sign-up, or fabricated scarcity.
4. **A world with purpose:** spatial and cinematic devices must help orientation, discovery, comprehension, or feedback.
5. **Story and evidence remain distinct:** creative narrative can be imaginative; factual claims need sources and qualification.
6. **One ecosystem, clear boundaries:** destinations feel related but retain their own jobs.
7. **Progressive disclosure:** reveal depth when the visitor asks for it; do not overwhelm the first screen.
8. **Accessible by design:** motion, sound, color, hover, and spatial metaphors never become access barriers.
9. **No fabricated proof:** no fake user counts, testimonials, rankings, live activity, uptime, or institutional endorsement.
10. **Useful exits:** every journey gives the visitor a clear next action and a way back.

## 6. What Home is not

- Not a conventional SaaS marketing page.
- Not a pricing page unless a real, approved offer exists.
- Not a dashboard of statistics.
- Not a game that blocks access until a cinematic sequence finishes.
- Not a portal that exposes private member or operator data.
- Not a substitute for the detailed MoonWitness archive or the authenticated applications.
- Not a claim that every concept shown is already implemented.

## 7. Success definition

The design succeeds when a first-time visitor can explain the project's purpose, distinguish the destinations, find a relevant next step, and understand what is public versus authenticated—without needing a guided tour.

The design also succeeds when a returning visitor can resume quickly, when mobile visitors can navigate without spatial gestures, and when every displayed claim can be traced to a source or is clearly framed as creative interpretation.

No numerical success target is defined here. Targets must be based on measured product analytics and approved privacy practices, not invented for the design.

## 8. Narrative tone

- Warm, deliberate, humble, curious, and cinematic.
- Clear Indonesian first; English localization should preserve meaning rather than translate word-for-word when that would sound unnatural.
- Playful Gen-Z voice may appear in story-specific contexts where it belongs, but public orientation and sacred/research content should not become flippant.
- Avoid empty marketing phrases such as “revolutionary”, “next-generation”, “seamless”, or “AI-powered” unless a concrete capability is explained.
- Use ALLAH consistently for divine referents according to the project's editorial convention; do not casually substitute other terms.

## 9. Content integrity

Every content object must carry a recognizable type and provenance:
- Scripture or religious source: exact reference and checked translation/interpretation.
- Hadith or historical report: source, grading/qualification where relevant, and context.
- Scientific or astronomical data: named source, observation/model distinction, timestamp, units, and uncertainty.
- Research hypothesis: explicit hypothesis label and evidence status.
- Personal reflection, dream, or narrative: clearly identified as personal/creative, not external proof.
- Fictional universe material: marked as story/worldbuilding where confusion is possible.

A visual coincidence, number pattern, timestamp, or personal experience is not scientific proof and must not be framed as such.

## 10. Scope and release boundary

This vision establishes product intent only. It does not approve implementation, content publication, legal claims, religious rulings, scientific conclusions, or production deployment. Those require their own evidence and review.
